Privacy Policy – Taka Reader

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Taka Reader (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) is an EPUB reading application designed to provide a comfortable and personalized reading experience, including customizable fonts, Liquid Glass and Material You interfaces, and supported book-listening features through Android Auto.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Taka Reader.

2. Information We Collect

Taka Reader is designed to provide its core reading features without requiring users to provide personal information, unless otherwise stated within the App.

Depending on the features you use and the services integrated into the App, the following information may be processed:

  • Book Files: EPUB files selected or imported by you for reading within the App.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as font styles, reading themes, display preferences, and other customization options.
  • Technical Information: Basic device or diagnostic information may be processed to maintain app functionality, troubleshoot errors, and improve stability, where applicable.
  • Third-Party Service Data: If third-party services such as analytics or advertising services are integrated, those providers may process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information for the App's core reading functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Taka Reader may be used to:

  • Open and display EPUB books selected by users.
  • Save reading preferences and provide a personalized interface.
  • Support compatible book-listening and Android Auto features.
  • Maintain app performance, stability, and compatibility.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the user experience.
  • Provide advertising or analytics functionality, if such services are enabled.

4. Book Files and Local Data

EPUB files and reading preferences may be accessed or stored locally on your device to provide the App's features.

Taka Reader does not require you to upload your personal book collection to a server for basic EPUB reading, unless a specific feature clearly indicates otherwise.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights to access and read the books you import.

5. Android Auto and Audio Features

Taka Reader may provide compatible book-listening functionality through Android Auto.

When using these features, the App may process book content, playback status, or related settings as necessary to provide the requested functionality. Data handling may also depend on Android, Android Auto, and any other services involved.

Please use audio features responsibly and follow applicable road safety laws while driving.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share personal information with third parties except where necessary to operate enabled app features, comply with applicable laws, protect our rights, or with your consent.

If third-party SDKs or services are integrated into the App, those services may collect or process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Reading preferences and other app-related data may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their own retention policies.

If the App introduces account-based or server-side features, additional procedures for requesting data deletion will be provided where required.

8. Children's Privacy

Taka Reader is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by law.

If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their devices secure and avoid importing files from untrusted sources.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party platforms or services for operating system integration, audio functionality, analytics, or advertising, if applicable.

These third parties may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review the relevant policies of those services.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device and the features available, you may be able to:

  • Manage access to files and other device permissions through system settings.
  • Change or reset reading preferences.
  • Remove imported files or delete locally stored app data.
  • Manage relevant advertising or privacy settings through your device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, applicable laws, or our data practices.

Any updated version will be published with a revised effective date.
